The Standard

Built on ISO/IEC 5259 — The International AI Data Quality Standard

ISO/IEC 5259 defines the global framework for AI data quality management — covering data collection, labeling, preprocessing, and verification. qualdata.ai implements all six parts of this standard, giving researchers and ML engineers a scientifically rigorous foundation.

View Official ISO/IEC 5259 Series on ISO.org
